Privacy & Data Use

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Workspace Dashboard accesses, why it is used, where it may be stored, and the choices available to users.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Contents

1. Overview

Workspace Dashboard is a web-based productivity dashboard that provides workspace organization, synchronization, account access controls, and administrative features. Some features use Google Sign-In and Google Drive APIs.

Workspace Dashboard uses Google user data only to provide and operate the features requested by the user.

2. Information we access

Depending on the features you use, Workspace Dashboard may access:

  • Basic Google Account profile information, such as your name, email address, profile image, and account identifier.
  • OAuth access information required to connect the application to Google services during an active session.
  • Workspace data created or managed through the dashboard, such as links, notes, schedules, groups, settings, favorites, recent items, and related application state.
  • Application account-management information such as role, access status, session state, and administrative audit information.

3. How Google user data is used

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used to identify the signed-in account, display basic profile information, associate workspace data with the correct user, and apply access-control rules.

Google Drive

Workspace Dashboard uses Google Drive to save, retrieve, synchronize, and maintain application data. Workspace data may be stored in the application's private Google Drive application-data area. The application also uses a shared Google Drive JSON registry file for account-management and administrative functions, including user roles, block status, session state, and related records.

Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling for advertising, or sale to third parties.

4. Storage and retention

Workspace information may be stored in Google Drive and in browser storage such as localStorage to support settings, session continuity, interface preferences, and application operation.

Data is retained for as long as needed to provide the application features, maintain account access, or until it is deleted by the user or administrator where the application provides that capability. Data stored in Google Drive remains subject to the user's or owner's Google Drive controls.

5. Data sharing

Workspace Dashboard does not sell Google user data or personal information. Data is not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Information may be processed by Google services when required to provide authentication and Google Drive functionality. Administrative account information may be visible to authorized Workspace Dashboard administrators where required for account-management features.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unauthorized access to application data. OAuth access tokens are used to communicate with Google APIs and should not be intentionally exposed to other users. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and control

You may stop using Workspace Dashboard at any time. You may also:

  • Sign out or disconnect your Google Account from the application.
  • Revoke Workspace Dashboard's access from your Google Account security and third-party access settings.
  • Delete application data that you control in Google Drive where applicable.
  • Contact the developer regarding privacy questions or data-access concerns.

8. Children's privacy

Workspace Dashboard is not specifically directed to children under 13. The application should only be used by persons permitted to use the relevant Google Account and services.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Workspace Dashboard features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
